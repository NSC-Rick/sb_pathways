# Business Transition Summary Prompt

## Purpose
This prompt will be used to generate AI-powered summaries of completed Business Transition workbooks for advisors.

## Context
When a client completes the Business Transition pathway, their workbook responses will be processed to create a concise snapshot that highlights:
- Transition goals and timeline
- Business readiness
- Key stakeholders and concerns
- Critical planning needs

## Prompt Template (Future Implementation)

```
You are reviewing a business transition workbook. Create a concise summary for an SBDC advisor that includes:

1. **Transition Goals** (2-3 sentences)
   - What type of transition? (sale, succession, closure)
   - What is the desired timeline?

2. **Business Readiness** (2-3 sentences)
   - Current state of the business
   - Preparation level for transition

3. **Key Considerations** (bullet list)
   - Stakeholder concerns (employees, family, etc.)
   - Financial or legal complexities
   - Emotional factors

4. **Advisor Focus Areas** (bullet list)
   - What planning is needed?
   - What decisions need guidance?

Keep the summary under 300 words. Be sensitive to emotional aspects of transition.
```

## Notes
- This will integrate with workbook submission system
- Transitions are often emotionally complex - tone matters
- Should identify if valuation, legal, or financial specialists needed
- Timeline urgency should be clearly flagged
