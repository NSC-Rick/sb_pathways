## Video Guidelines

### Production Standards
- Professional quality audio and video
- Clear, conversational presentation style
- Accessible captions/transcripts
- Mobile-friendly format

### Content Principles
- Actionable and practical
- Avoid jargon where possible
- Include real examples
- Supportive and encouraging tone

### Technical Specifications
- Format: MP4
- Recommended hosting: Vimeo or YouTube (unlisted)
- Embed directly in Streamlit pages
- Include download option for offline viewing
