# Workbook Content Ideas

## Purpose
This document outlines content ideas for each pathway workbook to guide future development.

---

## Idea Exploration Workbook

### Section 1: Your Business Concept
- What problem are you solving?
- Who experiences this problem?
- How do you solve it differently?
- Why now?

### Section 2: Target Market
- Who is your ideal customer?
- How many potential customers exist?
- Where do they currently get this solved?
- What would make them switch?

### Section 3: Competitive Landscape
- Who else solves this problem?
- What are their strengths?
- What are their weaknesses?
- What makes you different?

### Section 4: Revenue Model
- How will you make money?
- What will you charge?
- What are your costs?
- When will you break even?

### Section 5: Next Steps & Questions
- What are you most uncertain about?
- What do you need to validate first?
- What resources do you need?
- What questions do you have for your advisor?

---

## Loan Readiness Workbook

### Section 1: Loan Request
- How much do you need?
- What will you use it for?
- When do you need it?
- How will it help your business?

### Section 2: Financial Overview
- Current revenue and expenses
- Cash flow situation
- Existing debt
- Personal financial position

### Section 3: Collateral & Guarantees
- Assets available for collateral
- Personal guarantee willingness
- Co-signers or partners
- Other security

### Section 4: Business Case
- Why this loan makes sense
- How you'll repay it
- What happens if you don't get it
- Alternative funding explored

### Section 5: Documentation Checklist
- Financial statements prepared
- Tax returns available
- Business plan ready
- Supporting documents gathered

---

## Recovery & Stabilization Workbook

### Section 1: Current Situation
- What challenges are you facing?
- How long has this been happening?
- What have you tried?
- What's working/not working?

### Section 2: Cash Flow Analysis
- Current cash position
- Monthly burn rate
- Runway remaining
- Critical payment deadlines

### Section 3: Root Causes
- What led to this situation?
- What patterns do you see?
- What's within your control?
- What's external?

### Section 4: Potential Paths Forward
- Option 1: Continue as-is
- Option 2: Pivot or adjust
- Option 3: Scale back
- Option 4: Exit or close

### Section 5: Decision Framework
- What are your constraints?
- What are your priorities?
- What support do you need?
- What timeline are you working with?

---

## Business Transition Workbook

### Section 1: Transition Goals
- What type of transition? (sale, succession, closure)
- Why now?
- What's your ideal timeline?
- What's your ideal outcome?

### Section 2: Business Assessment
- Current business health
- Financial position
- Market position
- Growth potential

### Section 3: Readiness Evaluation
- Financial records organized
- Legal structure clear
- Operations documented
- Key relationships mapped

### Section 4: Stakeholder Considerations
- Employees
- Family members
- Partners or co-owners
- Customers and suppliers

### Section 5: Action Planning
- Immediate next steps
- 3-month priorities
- 6-month milestones
- Professional help needed

---

## Workbook Design Principles

### Format
- PDF fillable forms
- Clean, uncluttered layout
- Generous space for responses
- Professional but approachable design

### Tone
- Supportive and encouraging
- Clear and direct
- Non-judgmental
- Action-oriented

### Length
- 8-12 pages per workbook
- 2-3 hours to complete
- Can be done in multiple sessions
- Progress can be saved

### Accessibility
- Large, readable fonts
- High contrast
- Print-friendly
- Screen reader compatible
