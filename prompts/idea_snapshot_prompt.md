# Idea Snapshot Prompt

## Purpose
This prompt will be used to generate AI-powered summaries of completed Idea Exploration workbooks for advisors.

## Context
When a client completes the Idea Exploration pathway, their workbook responses will be processed to create a concise snapshot that highlights:
- Core business concept
- Target market insights
- Key assumptions and risks
- Critical questions for the advisor

## Prompt Template (Future Implementation)

```
You are reviewing a business idea exploration workbook. Create a concise summary for a business advisor that includes:

1. **Business Concept** (2-3 sentences)
   - What is the core idea?
   - What problem does it solve?

2. **Target Market** (2-3 sentences)
   - Who is the customer?
   - What market validation has been done?

3. **Key Assumptions** (bullet list)
   - What critical assumptions is this idea based on?

4. **Advisor Focus Areas** (bullet list)
   - What topics should the advisor prioritize?
   - What questions does the client need help with?

Keep the summary under 300 words. Be objective and highlight gaps or concerns.
```

## Notes
- This will integrate with workbook submission system
- Summary will be generated before advisor review
- Should flag incomplete sections or concerning gaps
