# Video Content Ideas

## Purpose
This document outlines video content ideas for each pathway to guide future production.

---

## General Video Principles

### Production Quality
- Professional but not corporate
- Clear audio (most important)
- Simple, clean visuals
- Accessible captions

### Presentation Style
- Conversational, not scripted
- Real examples and stories
- Practical and actionable
- Supportive tone

### Length Guidelines
- Introduction videos: 5-8 minutes
- Learning videos: 6-10 minutes
- Keep under 12 minutes maximum
- One concept per video

## Production Notes

### Filming Locations
- Professional but comfortable setting
- Good natural light
- Minimal background distractions
- Consistent across videos

### Presenters
- SBDC advisors (credibility)
- Successful business owners (relatability)
- Subject matter experts (depth)
- Mix of voices and perspectives

### Supporting Materials
- Downloadable transcripts
- Key takeaways summary
- Related resources
- Discussion prompts

### Distribution
- Embedded in Streamlit app
- Also available on YouTube (unlisted)
- Downloadable for offline viewing
- Mobile-optimized
