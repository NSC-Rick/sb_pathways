# Recovery & Stabilization Summary Prompt

## Purpose
This prompt will be used to generate AI-powered summaries of completed Recovery & Stabilization workbooks for advisors.

## Context
When a client completes the Recovery & Stabilization pathway, their workbook responses will be processed to create a concise snapshot that highlights:
- Current situation severity
- Cash flow status
- Viable options
- Urgency and constraints

## Prompt Template (Future Implementation)

```
You are reviewing a business recovery workbook. Create a concise, sensitive summary for an SBDC advisor that includes:

1. **Situation Overview** (2-3 sentences)
   - What challenges is the business facing?
   - How urgent is the situation?

2. **Financial Status** (2-3 sentences)
   - Current cash flow position
   - Runway and critical deadlines

3. **Potential Paths** (bullet list)
   - What options has the client identified?
   - What constraints exist?

4. **Advisor Focus Areas** (bullet list)
   - What decisions need to be made?
   - Where does the client need guidance?

Keep the summary under 300 words. Be honest but supportive in tone. Flag time-sensitive issues.
```

## Notes
- This will integrate with workbook submission system
- Tone should be supportive and non-judgmental
- Should flag urgent situations requiring immediate attention
- Advisor needs clear picture before meeting to prepare appropriately
