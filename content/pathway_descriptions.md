# Pathway Descriptions

## Purpose
This file contains detailed descriptions, use cases, and guidance for each pathway.

---

## Idea Exploration Pathway

### Overview
The Idea Exploration pathway is designed for aspiring entrepreneurs who have a business concept but need structured guidance to refine, validate, and prepare for launch.

### Ideal For
- First-time entrepreneurs with a business idea
- Individuals transitioning from employment to entrepreneurship
- People exploring side business opportunities
- Anyone needing to validate market demand

### Not Ideal For
- Businesses already operating (use Recovery or Transition pathways)
- Individuals seeking immediate funding without concept clarity (complete this first, then Loan Readiness)

### Expected Outcomes
By completing this pathway, clients will:
- Articulate their business concept clearly
- Understand their target market and competition
- Identify critical assumptions to test
- Prepare focused questions for their advisor
- Have a foundation for business planning

### Time Commitment
- Learning resources: 1-2 hours
- Workbook completion: 2-3 hours
- Total: 3-5 hours over 1-2 weeks

## Loan Readiness Pathway

### Overview
The Loan Readiness pathway helps business owners prepare comprehensive, compelling loan applications with organized financials and clear business cases.

### Ideal For
- Established businesses seeking growth capital
- Startups with traction seeking initial financing
- Businesses needing equipment or real estate loans
- Owners preparing for SBA loan applications

### Not Ideal For
- Pre-revenue startups (complete Idea Exploration first)
- Businesses in crisis (use Recovery & Stabilization first)
- Owners unclear on loan amount or purpose

### Expected Outcomes
By completing this pathway, clients will:
- Understand different loan types and requirements
- Organize all necessary financial documentation
- Build a compelling business case
- Identify gaps in their application
- Prepare for lender questions

### Time Commitment
- Learning resources: 2-3 hours
- Workbook completion: 3-4 hours
- Document gathering: varies
- Total: 5-8 hours over 2-3 weeks

## Recovery & Stabilization Pathway

### Overview
The Recovery & Stabilization pathway provides structured support for business owners facing operational or financial challenges who need objective assessment and strategic guidance.

### Ideal For
- Businesses experiencing cash flow problems
- Owners facing difficult strategic decisions
- Businesses needing to pivot or restructure
- Situations requiring objective third-party perspective

### Not Ideal For
- Businesses in immediate crisis requiring emergency intervention (contact advisor directly)
- Routine operational questions
- Businesses performing well but seeking growth

### Expected Outcomes
By completing this pathway, clients will:
- Assess their situation objectively
- Understand their options clearly
- Identify immediate priorities
- Prepare for difficult decisions
- Have a framework for moving forward

### Time Commitment
- Learning resources: 1-2 hours
- Workbook completion: 2-3 hours
- Total: 3-5 hours over 1 week

### Special Considerations
- This pathway is designed to be supportive and non-judgmental
- Confidentiality is paramount
- Advisors will be prepared for sensitive conversations
- Urgent situations will be prioritized

## Business Transition Pathway

### Overview
The Business Transition pathway guides owners through planning for sale, succession, or closure with clarity about options, timeline, and preparation steps.

### Ideal For
- Owners planning retirement
- Businesses considering sale or acquisition
- Family business succession planning
- Strategic closure or wind-down situations

### Not Ideal For
- Immediate crisis situations (use Recovery pathway)
- Routine business operations questions
- Owners not yet committed to transition exploration

### Expected Outcomes
By completing this pathway, clients will:
- Clarify transition goals and timeline
- Understand preparation requirements
- Assess business readiness for transition
- Identify key stakeholders and considerations
- Develop initial transition roadmap

### Time Commitment
- Learning resources: 2-3 hours
- Workbook completion: 3-4 hours
- Total: 5-7 hours over 2-3 weeks

### Special Considerations
- Transitions are often emotionally complex
- May require additional specialists (legal, financial, valuation)
- Timeline planning is critical
- Confidentiality considerations with employees and stakeholders

---

## Pathway Selection Guidance

### Multiple Pathways
Some clients may benefit from multiple pathways:
- **Idea → Loan**: Validate concept, then seek financing
- **Recovery → Transition**: Stabilize, then plan exit
- **Loan → Recovery**: If financing falls through and business struggles

### Pathway Switching
Clients can switch pathways if their situation changes or if initial selection wasn't optimal. Advisors can recommend pathway changes during meetings.

### Direct Advisor Access
While pathways provide structure, clients can always request direct advisor meetings for:
- Emergency situations
- Complex multi-faceted challenges
- Follow-up after pathway completion
